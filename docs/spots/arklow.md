# Arklow South Beach / The Cove

[Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/4dmHuTStYvu61gzv8)

**Works from:** SSW round through east to NNW.

## Seasons

- Spring sometimes brings north-westerlies that last for weeks.
- Summer is usually good, mostly southerly.
- Winter is often southerly, sometimes with a big shore break.

## Parking

| Parking | Wind |
|---|---|
| P1 | North-easterly (P2 also works and is better for beginners) |
| P2 | Southerly |
| P3 | Either direction, much more sheltered. Good at 20 kn+ or when the shore break is massive, thanks to the wind shadow from Arklow Rock |

## Hazards

- Arklow Rock makes a wind shadow at the south end.
- Fishing buoys, a good few of them, and sometimes they're connected to each other.
- Rocks along the shore: stay away from them.

## Landing and riding

- There are 4 safe landing spots along the beach. The 4th is safe to land on but can be tricky to walk back from.
- Off the north end there can be decent waves with nice drops.
- Further out, both southerly and northerly winds give plenty of bumps to play with.
